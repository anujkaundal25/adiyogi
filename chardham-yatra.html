The Divine Circuit

Explore The Four Sacred Dhams Of Uttarakhand

The 4 Dham Yatra Package tour is composed of the four holy dham, which symbolizes Faith, Devotion and Spiritual energy.

Yamunotri Dham

The first stop of the Char Dham Circuit is Yamunotri, dedicated to Goddess Yamuna. Nestled amid snow-capped peaks and pristine mountain scenery, it offers a serene atmosphere for devotion, meditation and quiet reflection for every pilgrim.

Gangotri Dham

Gangotri is one of the holiest destinations in Uttarakhand and marks the sacred origin of the holy River Ganga. Pilgrims visit this tranquil shrine to experience divine peace, take a dip in the icy waters and seek blessings in the spiritual Himalayan aura.

Kedarnath Dham

Kedarnath temple is a revered abode of Lord Shiva, perched at a high Himalayan elevation. A well-planned Kedarnath Tour Package ensures safe transport, comfortable accommodation and dedicated travel support for a smooth, blessed pilgrimage.

Badrinath Dham

The sacred Badrinath temple is dedicated to Lord Vishnu and ranks among India's most revered pilgrimage sites. A Kedarnath Badrinath tour package covers both shrines with organised travel, comfortable stay and a well-planned darshan itinerary.
